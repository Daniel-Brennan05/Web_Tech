About Hanover Tap

Hanover Tap is an amazing Craft Pub with a great range of craft beer, ales and craft cider. Hanover Tap provides an amazing atmosphere, delicious food and drinks. Plus the pub is pet-friendly and you are able to watch live sport games.

About the Quiz event

Hanover Tap offers a fun mix of trivia and great prizes for winners and awarsd for best team name. The quiz is run by Goose's Quizzes, they provide fun and well organaised events, making it a popular choice for students and locals.
