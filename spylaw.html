About The Spylaw

The Spylaw is located in the heart of old Colinton village. It is an amazing place for local commuunity and passers-by. This pub provides a freshly prepared food, an array of meals, ales, wines and whiskie collection. You’ll find this a relaxing, friendly place to enjoy a meal or just a good pint or glass of wine.

About the Quiz event

The Spylaw hosts a weekly pub quiz every Sunday and its run by Goose's Quizzes. Teams up to seven people can compete for the £30 gift voucher.




Quiz Details

  • Day: Sunday
  • Time: From 7.30pm
  • Entry: Free entry
  • Maximum people in team: 7
  • Prizes: 1st prize is a £30 gift voucher
