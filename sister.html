About The Three Sisters

The Three Sisters is a popular bar located at Cowfate, it offers live entertainment such as live sports and quizzes. The venue has a bunch of tasty food and drink options. The venue has recently undergone through a huge transformation especially within the main bar.

About the Quiz event

The Three Sisters hosts quizes every Tuesday in their main bar. Events feature brain teasers, bonus challanges and killer questions. There is also an accumulator round, it starts at £50 and increases by £50 each week if not won.
