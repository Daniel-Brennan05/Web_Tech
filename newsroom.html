About The Newsroom

The Newsroom is located in the centre of Edinburgh and it can be classified as a local hotspot. The establishment has relaxed vibe, enhanced by live music and comfortable seating. The drinks are beautifully crafted, the food is mouthwatering, and it’s all served with warm and friendly hospitality.
