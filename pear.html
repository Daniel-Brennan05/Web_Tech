About The Pear Tree

The Pear Tree is a pub, housed in one of Edinburgh's most historic buildings. The house was built in 1759. As you enter the door you will see two huge pear trees. They are now 170 years old and still flower and bare fruit each year.

About the Quiz event

The Pear Tree Quiz Nights are a lively weekly tradition full of quirky questions and friendly competition. Held in the cozy Pear Tree pub, they bring together locals and students for a fun, unpredictable evening of trivia.




Quiz Details

  • Time: 9.00pm - 11.00pm
  • Entry: £1 per person
